This Privacy Policy applies to DJ Pom-Pom and its associated services operated by the developer. DJ Pom-Pom is a third-party Discord application and is not operated by Discord.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use and the bot's configuration, DJ Pom-Pom may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user ID, server/guild ID, channel ID, role ID, and bot/application identifiers.
  • Server configuration: settings such as command permissions, designated music channels, prefixes or command configuration, language, volume defaults, and similar preferences.
  • Music interaction data: search terms, selected tracks, queue information, playlist/configuration information, and command interactions needed to provide music features.
  • Voice information: the voice-channel/server identifiers necessary to connect the bot to a voice channel and provide playback.
  • Technical/log information: timestamps, command names, error information, performance information, and security/rate-limit events where needed to operate and protect the service.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Need

DJ Pom-Pom does not need your Discord password, payment-card number, or private account credentials to provide ordinary bot functionality. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other secrets to the bot.
